* Geoscribe

** OVERVIEW
Geoscribe is a *geospatial metadata reader/writer*: it owns the metadata a
consumer like [[https://github.com/dxgeo/geosetta][Geosetta]] needs but can't
just carry through unchanged from a source — anything that has to be *resolved
or computed*, not copied. Two capabilities today:

- An embedded *CRS registry* — coordinate reference system definitions
  (PROJJSON + WKT1 + WKT2:2019) plus a name → =(authority, code)= index,
  covering *all 13,790 CRSes in PROJ's =proj.db== — every authority (EPSG,
  ESRI, IGNF, OGC, IAU_2015 planetary, PROJ, NKG) and type. The original
  reason this crate exists at all: PROJ/EPSG/Esri/IGN/IAU/NKG's data terms
  don't belong bundled into a pure-MIT library by default (see [[file:NOTICE][NOTICE]]), so
  the *data* lives here, isolated. Design:
  [[file:plans/crs-registry.org][plans/crs-registry.org]]. On top of the
  trusted-id lookup sits *identification*: recovering a CRS from an /id-less/
  WKT (an Esri-flavor Shapefile =.prj=, which carries no =AUTHORITY= node) by
  its name, validated against that WKT's own ellipsoid —
  [[file:plans/wkt-identify.org][plans/wkt-identify.org]].
Envelope (bounding-box) computation was briefly the planned second capability
here. *It was retired on 2026-08-18 without being implemented* and moved to
[[file:../geosetta/plans/envelope.org][geosetta/plans/envelope.org]] — see
[[file:plans/envelope-computation.org][plans/envelope-computation.org]]
§ STATUS. Its premise did not survive checking: =geosetta= already owns a
public =Bbox= fold and already writes GeoParquet's =geo= bbox from it, so the
"a value the consumer must derive, not one the source handed it" argument —
which is what broadened this charter in the first place — was describing a
capability the consumer already had.

Originally built as just the CRS registry for Geosetta; broadened on
2026-08-17 to *own whatever geospatial metadata isn't a simple pass-through*,
on the strength of that envelope argument. The broadening is *not* rescinded —
this crate is a metadata reader/writer, and =wkt-identify.org=/
=projjson-identify.org= are real instances of it that have nothing to do with
the CRS *table*. But the one worked example the broadening rested on turned out
to belong elsewhere, which is worth keeping in view: "isn't a simple
pass-through" is a test a capability can pass while still being in the wrong
crate. Ask separately whether the consumer already has it, and whether it can
reach this crate at all
([[file:../architecture.org][../architecture.org]]: there are no dependency
edges between the three crates). Kept as a standalone, reusable crate rather
than data/logic owned by Geosetta itself.

** ARCHITECTURE
Geoscribe is a *peer* of =geosetta= and =nazca=, not a layer beneath them.
As of 2026-08-18 there are no dependency edges between any pair of the three
crates, in either direction — see
[[file:../architecture.org][../architecture.org]] § THE COMPOSITION MODEL for
why, and for the two boundary rules that govern it. (An earlier framing called
this crate the graph's "shared leaf" that the other two optionally depended
on; =geosetta= dropped that dependency in its 0.24.0 and =nazca= dropped its
planned one on 2026-08-18.)

Nothing about this crate's job changes as a result — the consumers are simply
*callers* rather than *dependents*: a program that links =geoscribe= alongside
whatever else it needs, or a shell pipeline that runs its CLI. Priorities, in
order:

- *Own the metadata so consumers don't re-derive it.* Decoding (zstd) and
  lookup are owned *here* (=resolve=/=resolve_by_name=/=all=), not shipped as
  a raw blob each consumer must decode itself. =geosetta='s own copy of that
  logic was deleted when it switched to calling through
  ([[file:plans/public-api.org][plans/public-api.org]]); =nazca= never grew
  one, because with no edge it never sees the blob at all.
- *Text at the boundary.* =resolve()= returns a =CrsRecord= of *strings*
  (PROJJSON, WKT, WKT2), not a parsed Rust struct — consumers parse into their
  own representation on their own side. These are external, spec-defined
  formats neither crate owns, so there is nothing to couple on.
- *No external dependencies.* Including the zstd decoder, which is this
  crate's own from-scratch port, deliberately independent of =geosetta='s —
  documented duplication, not an oversight
  ([[file:plans/public-api.org][plans/public-api.org]] § MODULE LAYOUT).
- *Keep the consumer pure-MIT by default.* This is why the data is isolated
  here at all. =geosetta= took it further in its 0.24.0 and dropped the
  dependency altogether — its =crs-registry= feature is gone — composing with
  this crate over a *pipe* instead (=geosetta --print-crs-code= /
  =--crs <path|->=). Nothing here assumes a consumer links against it; the CLI
  is a first-class interface, not a convenience wrapper.
- *Answer, or say why not — never guess.* The trusted-id path
  (=resolve=) is exact. Everything weaker states its evidence: =resolve_by_name=
  returns candidates and validates nothing, =identify_from_wkt= validates
  against the input's own ellipsoid and reports [[file:plans/wkt-identify.org][ambiguity]]
  rather than picking one of several equally-supported answers by sort order.
- *Generated, pinned, oracle-gated.* The embedded data is generated from
  =proj.db= by =tools/gen_crs_registry.py=, never hand-edited, and gated on an
  oracle: every embedded definition must round-trip at =projinfo --identify=.

** STATUS
*Published under this name.* The crate began life as =geosetta-crs-data= and
was renamed to =geoscribe= ([[https://github.com/dxgeo/geoscribe][GitHub]]); the
rename is complete on both ends — releases go out under the new name on
[[https://crates.io/crates/geoscribe][crates.io]], and every
=geosetta-crs-data= version is yanked, pointing dependents here.

The CRS registry is *done* (=crs-registry.org= R1–R5, =public-api.org= R6a–R6d),
as is id-less identification in *both* dialects (=wkt-identify.org=, and
=projjson-identify.org= as of 2026-08-19 — which also closed the cross-repo item
with =geosetta='s =--print-crs=). Envelope computation was retired out of this
crate on 2026-08-18 without being implemented. Nothing is open. See
[[file:plans/README.org][plans/README.org]] for the plan index and
[[file:plans/todo.org][plans/todo.org]] for what's open right now. A session
picking up the CRS work should start at [[file:handoff.org][handoff.org]].

** IMPLEMENTATION
- =src/lib.rs= :: the public surface — =resolve=, =resolve_by_name=, =all=,
  =identify=, =identify_from_wkt=, =identify_from_projjson=, =CrsRecord=,
  =Identity=, =Evidence=, =CRS_COUNT=, =DATASET_VERSIONS=.
- =src/registry.rs= :: =GCR1= decode + lookup internals (binary search over a
  sorted fixed-width index; decode-once behind a =OnceLock=).
- =src/zstd.rs= :: this crate's own zstd decoder (RFC 8878, from scratch).
- =src/identify.rs= :: name lookup validated against the input's own ellipsoid,
  returning =Identity::{Unique, Ambiguous, Unidentified}=. The trust policy that
  sits *on top of* =resolve_by_name=, and the one place in this crate that
  weighs evidence rather than reporting it
  ([[file:plans/wkt-identify.org][plans/wkt-identify.org]],
  [[file:plans/projjson-identify.org][plans/projjson-identify.org]]).
  =identify_from_wkt= and =identify_from_projjson= differ only in extraction —
  steps 2 and 3 are one shared implementation — and =identify= sniffs the
  dialect, prefers an inline id when the definition states one, and reports
  which =Evidence= produced the answer.
- =src/wkt.rs=, =src/json.rs= :: a shallow WKT lexer and a minimal read-only
  JSON reader, both feeding =identify.rs= only. Ported rather than shared —
  same reasoning as the zstd decoder; see each module's header.
- =src/main.rs= :: the =geoscribe= CLI — the trusted-id lookup, plus
  =--identify= for a definition in either dialect (sniffed, never flagged, so it
  cannot collide with the =--wkt=/=--wkt2=/=--projjson= *output* flags). Reports
  its evidence on one line of stderr, leaving stdout to the definition alone.
  Still never exposes the raw, unvalidated =resolve_by_name=.
- =tests/= :: the Esri corpus (431 geographic + 2,274 projected real
  =WKT1_ESRI= exports) behind =identify_esri.rs=; the CLI's stdout/stderr/
  exit-status contracts behind =cli_identify.rs=; and =identify_projjson_oracle.rs=,
  which strips the root =id= from all 13,790 embedded definitions and feeds each
  back in — a complete id-less corpus with known answers, needing no fixtures and
  no =projinfo=.
- =src/generated.rs=, =src/names.rs=, =src/registry.bin.zst= :: generated —
  the =GCR1= v2 blob (1.07 MB compressed, 13,790 CRSes), the name →
  =(authority, code)= index (20,760 entries), and the stamped sizes/versions.
- =tools/= :: build-time generators; need PROJ's =projinfo= and =proj.db=.
  Container format: [[file:plans/registry-format.org][plans/registry-format.org]].

** USAGE
As a library:

#+begin_src rust
let rec = geoscribe::resolve("EPSG", "4326").expect("EPSG:4326 present");
assert!(rec.wkt.is_some());  // None for the ~4% of entries WKT1 can't express
assert!(rec.wkt2.is_some()); // present for effectively every entry

for (authority, code) in geoscribe::resolve_by_name("GCS_WGS_1984") {
    // multiple authorities can share a catalog name — this is a raw lookup
    // that validates nothing; see plans/public-api.org § BOUNDARY
}

// Or let the crate do the validating: identify an id-less .prj by name,
// checked against its own ellipsoid. Never guesses, never picks.
match geoscribe::identify_from_wkt(&std::fs::read_to_string("parcels.prj")?) {
    geoscribe::Identity::Unique(rec) => println!("{}:{}", rec.authority, rec.code),
    geoscribe::Identity::Ambiguous(recs) => { /* several fit equally well */ }
    geoscribe::Identity::Unidentified => { /* nothing fits — decline */ }
}
#+end_src

As a CLI — writes exactly the requested definition to stdout, nothing else, so
it composes with anything that wants a WKT/PROJJSON string on a pipe:

#+begin_src sh
geoscribe EPSG:4326                # PROJJSON (default — the one every entry has)
geoscribe EPSG:3857 --wkt2         # WKT2:2019
geoscribe OGC:CRS84 --wkt          # WKT1 (GDAL flavor); errors if absent
ogr2ogr -a_srs $(geoscribe EPSG:4326) ...
#+end_src

Exit =1= with a message on stderr for an unknown code or a requested dialect
the CRS doesn't have.

=--identify= covers what an authority code can't: a definition that states no
code, read from a file or stdin, in *either dialect* — WKT or PROJJSON, sniffed
on the first non-whitespace byte rather than flagged, since =--wkt=/=--wkt2=/
=--projjson= already mean the *output* dialect. It is the same output contract —
the definition alone on stdout — so it drops straight into a pipeline:

#+begin_src sh
geoscribe --identify --projjson parcels.prj \
  | geosetta parcels.shp parcels.parquet --crs -
#+end_src

The PROJJSON half is what reaches a CRS buried in a container, which is every
format but a Shapefile =.prj=. Paired with =geosetta --print-crs=, one
unconditional command covers it:

#+begin_src sh
geosetta parcels.parquet --print-crs \
  | geoscribe --identify --projjson \
  | geosetta parcels.parquet parcels.fgb --crs -
#+end_src

*Unconditional* because =--identify= prefers an inline =id= when the definition
states one, so the caller never has to know whether it does; which evidence was
used is reported on one line of stderr, leaving stdout untouched.

This is *weaker evidence* than a stated code (a name plus an ellipsoid), so it
declines rather than guessing, and where several real CRSes fit equally well it
writes *nothing* to stdout, lists them on stderr, and exits =2=:

#+begin_src sh
$ geoscribe --identify utm.prj
geoscribe: 2 CRSes match this WKT's name and ellipsoid equally well;
re-run with the one you want (or --all to list them):
  EPSG:6339
  ESRI:102057
$ geoscribe --identify --all utm.prj    # the codes alone, for adjudication
EPSG:6339
ESRI:102057
$ geoscribe EPSG:6339 --wkt2            # then resolve the one you chose
#+end_src

An empty stdout is deliberate: =geosetta --crs -= hard-errors on an empty
override, so an ambiguous or failed identification fails the pipeline loudly
instead of quietly writing a mislabeled file.

** ROADMAP
- [[file:plans/public-api.org][plans/public-api.org]] *R6e* — the acceptance
  test: a consumer with no =geosetta= dependency resolving a real code through
  this crate alone. Open. The vehicle is now a throwaway smoke-test crate (or
  a =dev-dependency= test on =nazca='s side), since =nazca= no longer carries
  a path-dep on this crate to exercise.
- *Ambiguity is common enough to be worth revisiting* — over the Esri corpus,
  110 of 431 geographic and 891 of 2,274 projected =.prj= exports match more
  than one registry CRS equally well (typically a deprecated ESRI code beside
  its live EPSG twin). The blob carries no deprecation flag, so nothing here
  can prefer one honestly today; adding that to =GCR1= would resolve most of
  them. See [[file:plans/wkt-identify.org][plans/wkt-identify.org]] § OPEN QUESTIONS.
- ~~[[file:plans/envelope-computation.org][plans/envelope-computation.org]]~~ —
  *retired 2026-08-18, moved out of this crate and never implemented here.* The
  envelope fold and the GeoPackage writer gap are now
  [[file:../geosetta/plans/envelope.org][geosetta/plans/envelope.org]];
  transforming a bbox across CRSes is
  [[file:../nazca/plans/transform-bounds.org][nazca/plans/transform-bounds.org]].
  The Cargo-feature question that gated it is moot — no crate has a feature
  gating a sibling crate any more.
- *Registry currency* — the embedded data is only as current as the =proj.db=
  it was generated from; a newer EPSG release means regenerating *and*
  re-checking attribution ([[file:plans/crs-registry.org][plans/crs-registry.org]]
  § ATTRIBUTION). No trigger for this exists today.
- *Charter discipline* — "own whatever geospatial metadata isn't a simple
  pass-through" is an easy bar to satisfy sloppily, and envelope computation is
  now the worked example of *how*: =envelope-computation.org= did ask "why here
  and not in =geosetta=" explicitly, and still landed wrong, because it answered
  from the charter rather than from the code. Two checks that plan did not run,
  which the next capability must:
  1. *Does the consumer already have it?* =geosetta= had a public =Bbox= fold
     writing GeoParquet's =geo= bbox the whole time.
  2. *Can the consumer reach this crate at all?* It cannot — there are no
     dependency edges between the three crates
     ([[file:../architecture.org][../architecture.org]]), so a capability
     planned "for =geosetta='s benefit" has no delivery path by construction.
  =nazca='s =project.org= § CHARTER is the model for what a bounded charter
  looks like: a falsifiable test ("does this need to know *which* CRS the
  coordinates are in?"), not a category.

*Non-goals*, unchanged: reprojection / coordinate transformation (that's
=nazca=), embedding the *transformation* dataset (Helmert/grid pipelines —
only CRS *definitions* are embedded), and any live runtime dependency on
PROJ/GDAL.
